## Overview

AESE is a Portuguese business school with campuses in Lisbon and Porto. It offers an executive MBA,
executive programmes, short courses and custom programmes for companies. With that many kinds of
programme, the old website could not present them all in a consistent way.

The new website is a Buzzvel project. Buzzvel organised the information and created a content model
shared by every programme, and its design team designed the pages. The site is a custom WordPress
theme, in Portuguese and English.

## My part

I developed the theme's front end. That meant turning the Buzzvel design team's work into Gutenberg
blocks, with their editable fields in ACF, so AESE's team can build and update pages from those
blocks.

The theme uses what WordPress already provides and adds plugins where it made sense:

- **Custom post types** for programmes, faculty and documents, each with its own ACF fields. A new
  programme enters the site with the same structure as the others.
- **WordPress's own APIs** for the forms and for the listings of programmes, faculty and documents.
- **Fluent Forms** for form submissions, managing registrations for events in the agenda and
  registering faculty members.
- **WPML** to translate the site between Portuguese and English.

## Results and lessons

The site is live at aese.pt. According to Buzzvel's case study, the new model makes the school's
editorial work easier and lets it add programmes without rebuilding the site's structure.
