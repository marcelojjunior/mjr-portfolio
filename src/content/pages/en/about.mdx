My name is Marcelo Junior. I'm a software engineer, I live in São Paulo, and I have spent five years
building systems, websites and apps.

I started in IT support, in Balsas, in the north-east of Brazil. For a year and a half I fixed
hardware and software incidents, managed users and permissions in Active Directory, and ran database
queries to answer questions coming from other departments. That is where I decided I would rather
build the system than keep an existing one standing.

I came into development through the front end, at Buzzvel, working remotely for a company based in
Lisbon. Over a little more than two years I worked on landing pages, internal systems, institutional
sites and custom WordPress themes. I then led the team for eight months, looking after sprints, code
reviews and the developers themselves.

I moved to the back end once I realised most of the problems I was trying to fix in a component had
already been born wrong in the API. I work at both ends now, and that is the most useful thing about
what I do: I can look at a slow screen and tell whether the problem is rendering, a query, or the
data model.

I mostly work with **Laravel**, **React**, **Next.js** and **React Native**, on top of
**PostgreSQL** or **Firebase**. The technology list is the least interesting part, because anyone a
few years into this job has a similar one. What travels with me from project to project is the habit
of understanding the business before choosing the architecture, and of writing a decision down
together with what it costs.

Outside work, I build projects to solve problems for people close to me. The most recent one is the
information hub for the graduation of a medical class of 140 students.
